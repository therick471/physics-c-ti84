MODEL CONDITIONS
All geometry is idealized and electrostatic. Infinite cylinders/sheets
neglect end/edge effects. Spherical boundary field uses the outside limit.
Energy speed is nonrelativistic and assumes fixed sources and no other work.
Charge sharing applies to identical isolated conductors at mutual equilibrium.
Numerical solvers do not replace the derivation/reasoning required on paper.
Scope is based on Canvas notes 8.1-6 and 9.1, including electric potential.
Exact exam questions, calculator permissions and reference policy are unverified.

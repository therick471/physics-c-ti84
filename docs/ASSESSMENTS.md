## Quiz 8.4 checklist

For the finite rod: use L as full length, dq=λ dy, distance √(x²+y²), the projection x/√(x²+y²), bounds −L/2 to +L/2, and the substitution y=x tanθ. The result is E=kQ/[x√(x²+(L/2)²)] for x>0, directed away from a positive rod.

For the ring: show transverse cancellation and integrate the axial projection to get E_x=kQx/(R²+x²)^(3/2). Check x=0, x=R and far away; the positive-side maximum for Q>0 is at x=R/√2. Final-formula browsing helps check the result; it does not replace the proof.
